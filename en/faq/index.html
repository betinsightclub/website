Frequently Asked Questions

BetInsight is a platform for football analysis, structured tips, unit weightings, documented results, statistics, calculators, live formats and community features. The BetInsight ecosystem also includes TIME CLASH, an interactive football entertainment simulation for historical and hypothetical matchups.

Is BetInsight a bookmaker?

No. BetInsight is an analysis, information and experience platform and does not accept sports bets.

Is BetInsight an online casino?

No. BetInsight itself is not an online casino. Internal units are platform units, not casino chips.

Can I use BetInsight for free?

Yes. Registered users can claim free internal units every day and use them for selected platform features, such as unlocking tips.

What are BetInsight units?

Units are internal platform units. Registered users can claim free internal units every day and use them, for example, to unlock tips. Units are not a profit guarantee.

Does BetInsight show only winning tips in its statistics?

No. The statistics include completed winning and losing tips. Losses are part of the documented record just like wins.

How are cancelled matches handled?

If a user has already unlocked a tip and used units for it, those units are credited back 100% when the match is cancelled or not graded. The user does not lose those units.

How much experience do the BetInsight tipsters have?

BetInsight works with two experienced tipsters. One of them has been intensively involved with sports betting and football analysis for around 11 years and spent about 5 of those years working in the field professionally. The second tipster also brings many years of practical experience to the analysis work.

Do I need a paid subscription to use BetInsight?

No. BetInsight can also be used without a Premium membership. Premium and Premium Plus are optional memberships that unlock additional helpful features, but they are not required to use BetInsight.

What do I get after registering?

After registration, each user gets access to a personal BetInsight area where available tips can be viewed and unlocked with internal units. Each user also automatically receives a trainer profile for TIME CLASH. The free version currently includes 5 game runs per week; additional runs can be unlocked by using internal units. Registered users can also publish posts in the social area for the BetInsight community. Premium and Premium Plus remain optional.

Where can I get help if something does not work?

Users can contact support through the integrated support and ticket system. Help is also available via Telegram and regular webinar or live formats. For selected issues or more personal exchange, a Founder Call can also be arranged.

Who operates BetInsight?

BetInsight is a digital project of the newly founded LucMedia LTDA. The platform was created by a founder with long-standing experience in developing and implementing own projects, online communities, websites, advertising materials and digital concepts. Practical experience from working with groups, teams and users has been incorporated into BetInsight. The founder also appears regularly in webinars and live formats and is in direct exchange with the community.

What is TIME CLASH?

TIME CLASH is BetInsight's interactive simulation and entertainment area. Its results are not predictions for real-world betting.

Is BetInsight an AI betting platform?

No. BetInsight should not be understood as an AI bookmaker. Technical and AI-assisted functions may support individual areas; TIME CLASH remains an entertainment simulation and is kept separate from real tips.

Does BetInsight guarantee profits?

No. Analyses, weightings and statistics do not guarantee any particular sports result.

How do I sign in to BetInsight?

After registration you receive an email with a personal access code. Open the member area and enter the code in the indicated field. Never share your access code.

Where can I find an illustrated guide?

Open the “First steps” page to view the detailed PDF guide. You can also find it in your member area and Academy.

How can I buy Units?

Direct BetInsight package purchases are paid exclusively with cryptocurrency. In the peer-to-peer Unit Exchange, bank transfers may be available if the seller expressly accepts them.

Where can I see my unlocked tips?

Open “Unlocked Tips” in your member area to view previously unlocked content again.

Can I change the language?

Yes. The app has a language selector. On the “First steps” page you can also choose your guide directly in one of six languages.

How do I contact support?

Sign in and open “Support”. You can submit a private support ticket and read replies to existing requests.

What types of Units are there?

Purchase Units come from packages, Gift Units from approved promotions, and Referral Units from your network. Not all types are sellable.

Which Units are spent first?

Available Gift Units are used before Purchase Units. Spending is recorded on your account.

Where can I check Unit transactions?

Open balance and activities in your dashboard to check credited and spent Units.

How late can I unlock a tip?

Always check the kick-off time. Tips cannot be newly unlocked after a match begins.

Where can I find tip results and evidence?

Open your unlocked or historical tips to see available results, status information and evidence.

Why can't I sell some Units?

Only Units explicitly released for sale can be sold. Expected Referral Units and Gift Units are not automatically sellable.

What does the 48-hour reservation mean?

A reserved offer has a 48-hour period for the required payment confirmation. Follow the offer instructions and submit proof only in the designated area.

When are expected Referral Units released?

Expected amounts are not instantly withdrawable. Release depends on programme rules and actual use of eligible Purchase Units. Compare expected and released balances in your network overview.

What is the difference between Basic, Premium and Premium Plus?

Basic provides core features. Premium and Premium Plus add features such as provider and odds information. See Membership for the current benefits.

Where are Daily Bonus and Fan Challenge?

Both are in the app menu under Free Units. Any rewards depend on the relevant conditions.

Why must I add a wallet or payment method when selling Units?

In the Exchange, buyers pay sellers directly. Use your own receiving details and check the crypto network. BetInsight does not collect payment for sellers.

What if Units or a commission are missing?

Refresh and check transaction history first. If the discrepancy remains, submit a support ticket with the date and details. Never share your access code.

Are there webinars?

Yes. BetInsight runs a workshop every Wednesday at 20:00 Germany time and a live webinar every Thursday at 20:00 Germany time. Both are held in German and appear in the same event calendar, where you can also find webinar registration. If you prefer not to register for webinar emails, you can check the dates in our Telegram channel.
