What if football history could be played again?

The NYC-Manhattan publication introduces TIME CLASH as a football simulation and entertainment experience built around historical and hypothetical matchups across different eras.

Different eras. One matchup.

TIME CLASH lets football fans explore questions that real football history can never answer directly: What would happen if famous teams, generations or national sides from different periods met in one simulated series?

What the NYC publication highlights

  • Teams from different eras: historical and modern football sides can be compared in one fictional matchup.
  • International football: national and club football scenarios can cross countries and generations.
  • Data, history and comparisons: the experience combines football context with structured comparisons.
  • Simulated match series: TIME CLASH presents fictional series rather than claiming a real historical result.
  • Stadium experience: the concept includes visual stadium settings and match presentation.

Example idea

Brazil vs Italy. Past vs present. Different generations. One matchup. The purpose is to explore football “What if?” scenarios as entertainment, not to reproduce an actual historical match.
