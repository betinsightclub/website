Why Hit Rate Alone Doesn't Tell the Whole Story

An English-language Parler post comparing betting hit rates, odds and actual returns.

About the published post

The promotional infographic illustrates why judging football picks by hit rate alone can be misleading. A higher win rate does not automatically translate into better financial performance.

Example A shows a 70% hit rate at average odds of 1.25, yielding a theoretical loss of 12.5 units across 100 equal-stake selections. Example B shows a 48% hit rate at average odds of 2.30 and a theoretical gain of 10.4 units. Both examples are hypothetical calculations, not verified BetInsight performance.

Factors such as odds at release, ROI, yield, stake size and sample size matter when assessing published predictions.
