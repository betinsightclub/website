Two separate ways to participate

BetInsight combines structured sports analysis with two separate network opportunities. Members can decide for themselves how deeply they want to participate.

1. BetInsight Unit Network – free to join

Every registered member can receive a personal referral link and build a network across up to 3 levels.

  • free participation
  • personal referral link
  • up to 3 network levels
  • Referral Units based on actual activity
  • Units can be used within the BetInsight system

Referral Units are not awarded simply because someone registers. They are generated when referred members actually use purchased Units within BetInsight.

2. BetInsight Premium Network

Members who want to build a larger partner network can optionally activate one of the Premium plans.

  • Premium: network participation up to 5 levels
  • Premium Plus: network participation up to 8 levels
  • eligible Premium members can participate in commissions generated through paid Premium memberships within their network

The Premium Network is separate from the free Unit Network, so members can decide for themselves how far they want to participate.

Why BetInsight?

  • international sports-analysis platform
  • transparent results and performance history
  • accessible worldwide
  • personal referral system
  • free start through the basic network

BetInsight is not a bookmaker and does not accept bets. It is a digital members platform for sports analysis, tips and network participation.
