What if the greatest football teams from different eras could really play each other?

The new English-language TIME CLASH feature on Medium explores dream matchups across football history — and the ideas surrounding BetInsight's expanding virtual football universe.

Platform: MediumLanguage: EnglishReported publication: 10 November 2026International audience

Football history, reimagined

What would happen if legendary squads could meet outside the limits of their own time? TIME CLASH invites fans to explore that question through fictional, simulated football encounters, not through claims about real-world results.

The English feature published on Medium introduces the idea of teams from different football generations playing one another. Two of the featured examples are:

🇩🇪Germany 1974 vs Germany 2014

Two World Cup-winning generations, with very different styles and eras.

🇧🇷 🇪🇸Brazil 2002 vs Spain 2010

A hypothetical meeting of two celebrated national-team generations.

Teams and players from around the world

The article broadens the concept beyond Europe, exploring national teams and clubs from South America, Africa, Asia and other football regions. It also discusses player-selection questions when a footballer appears in multiple eligible squads or eras. These are creative roster choices within a fictional format.

More than a virtual match

  • Stadium World: a wider experience built around stadiums, maps and virtual football settings.
  • Penalty Clash V32: another BetInsight football experiment covered in the article.
  • Music experiments: German- and Brazilian-language TIME CLASH music explorations.
  • Project development: the article also mentions ways readers may voluntarily support further TIME CLASH development.

These topics reflect the scope described for the Medium feature. This archive overview is not a reproduction of the full Medium article; visit the original for the complete text and its own wording.
