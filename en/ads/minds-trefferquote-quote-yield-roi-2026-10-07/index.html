Hit Rate Alone Says Little: Understanding Odds, Yield and ROI

An English Minds post from October 7 about the difference between win rates and betting performance.

About the published post

The original English post explains that a high hit rate does not automatically mean stronger performance. “70% can still lose. 48% can still win.”

The infographic compares equal-stake hypothetical examples: 70 out of 100 selections at average odds of 1.25 lead to −12.5 units, while 48 out of 100 at average odds of 2.30 produce +10.4 units.

The post highlights odds at release, ROI, yield and sample size. These are mathematical illustrations, not historical audited results from BetInsight.
