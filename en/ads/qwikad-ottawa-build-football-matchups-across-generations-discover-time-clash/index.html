Football beyond today’s fixtures

TIME CLASH turns “what if?” football questions into an interactive simulation experience. Fans can create matchups between teams from different generations and explore how contrasting eras and football styles might meet.

What the Ottawa publication highlights

  • Historical and international teams: select teams from different periods and countries.
  • Create matchups: build fictional football encounters that never happened in real life.
  • Compare eras and styles: explore differences between generations and football approaches.
  • Simulated encounters and series: follow fictional matches and multi-game series inside TIME CLASH.
  • Football discovery: go beyond the current fixture list and explore broader football history.

Who would you choose?

A legendary national team from the past — or today’s stars? TIME CLASH is designed around exactly these cross-generation football questions.
