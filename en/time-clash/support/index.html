The project

What is TIME CLASH?

TIME CLASH is an interactive football entertainment project bringing historical national teams and imagined cross-generation matchups into a new simulation world. We are developing the experience step by step — it is not being presented as a finished product.

Who operates TIME CLASH?

TIME CLASH is developed under BetInsight.club and operated by LucMedia LTDA, a for-profit technology and media company based in Brazil. Contributions support the development of this independent entertainment project; they are voluntary and do not purchase betting tips, units, memberships, equity, financial returns or guaranteed rewards.

LucMedia LTDA is not a registered charity. No charitable donation tax receipt is issued. Please verify any crypto network and receiving address before making a payment. This page does not collect payment keys or request wallet recovery phrases.
